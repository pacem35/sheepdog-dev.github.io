Muster

Tasks, calendar and alarms in one place. A personal Android app by Sheepdog_Dev.

Tasks & lists

Quick to-dos with reminders, repeating tasks and separate lists for things like vehicle work.

Google Calendar

Shows your upcoming events, can add your dated tasks to your calendar, and can set alarms before events you choose, such as shifts.

Alarms that get you up

Custom sounds and optional challenges you have to finish before the alarm turns off.

Muster has no servers and no accounts of its own. Everything stays on your phone, and it only talks to Google Calendar when you connect it.
