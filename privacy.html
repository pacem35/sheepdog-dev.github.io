Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Muster is a personal Android app for tasks, calendar events and alarms, made by Sheepdog_Dev. This policy explains what data Muster uses and where it goes. The short version: Muster has no servers. Your data stays on your phone and is only shared with Google when you connect Google Calendar.

Data Muster keeps on your phone

  • Your tasks, lists, notes, reminders, alarms and settings.
  • Sound files you choose for alarms.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: your sign-in tokens and a copy of your upcoming events, so the app works without a connection.

This data is stored in Muster's private storage on your device. Muster does not send it to the developer or anyone else. Google sign-in tokens and the copy of your calendar are excluded from Android backups.

Google user data

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Muster asks Google for permission to:

  • See your email address, to show which account is connected.
  • See your list of calendars, so you can choose which ones to show and use.
  • View and edit events, to show your upcoming events in the app, set alarms before events you choose, add your dated tasks to a calendar you pick, update or remove those task events when you change the task, and apply changes you make to those events in Google Calendar back to the task.

Muster only uses this data to provide those features to you inside the app. It does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not share or transfer it to any third party, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your data.

Muster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Muster has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting services and no tracking.

Permissions on your phone

Muster uses Android permissions only for its own features: notifications for reminders, exact alarms, showing the alarm over the lock screen, internet access to reach Google Calendar, and optional access to calendars stored on the phone.

Removing your data

  • In Muster, Settings → Calendar → Google Calendar → Sign out disconnects your Google account and revokes Muster's access. You can choose to remove the task events Muster added.
  • You can also remove Muster's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Muster deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on Muster's Google sign-in screen.
